Every trip on this page is measured against this one, because it isn't
really a trip. It's where day one happened.

## Why it mattered

Everything about how I think — stubborn, direct, allergic to wasted
motion — traces back to growing up here. It's easy to romanticize a
hometown once you've left it for a while; I try not to, but I also don't
pretend it didn't shape every decision that came after.

## Interesting facts

- One of the youngest capital cities in Europe, of one of its youngest
  countries.
- Home to a population that treats hospitality as a near non-negotiable
  house rule.

## Recommendations

If you visit, let someone feed you before you ask where the good coffee
is. They'll insist either way.
