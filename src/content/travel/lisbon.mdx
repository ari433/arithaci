Lisbon was supposed to be a four-day trip for a conference. I stayed
eleven.

## Why it mattered

It was the first city where I worked from a different desk every
morning and still shipped on schedule — the first proof that the work
didn't need to live in one place.

## Interesting facts

- Lisbon is built across seven hills, and the trams are not exaggerating
  about the incline.
- It's one of the oldest cities in Western Europe, older than London,
  Paris, and Rome.

## Recommendations

Get up early enough to see Alfama before the tour groups do. The best
work I did all week happened before 8am, in a café that didn't open its
kitchen until nine.
