I went to Tokyo expecting to be impressed by the technology. I left
thinking about attention to detail in everything else instead.

## Why it mattered

Every small business I visited — a six-seat ramen counter, a stationery
shop the size of a closet — operated with a level of care I hadn't seen
applied to something so small. It changed how I think about what
"finished" means in my own work.

## Interesting facts

- Tokyo's train system moves over a billion passengers a year and still
  apologizes publicly for delays measured in seconds.
- The city has more Michelin-starred restaurants than any other in the
  world.

## Recommendations

Skip the itinerary on day one. Get lost in a neighborhood with no
plan — that's where everything worth remembering happened for me.
