## Problem

Given 48 hours and a stage at the end of it, most teams built demos.
I wanted to ship something a judge could actually use during the demo
itself, not just watch.

## Solution

A lightweight AI copilot that judges could interact with live, built to
survive an unreliable conference wifi connection and a five-minute demo
slot with zero room for a crash.

## Timeline

- **Hour 0–6** — Scoped down from four ideas to one.
- **Hour 6–36** — Built, broke, rebuilt the core loop three times.
- **Hour 40** — Final demo script, rehearsed until it was boring.
- **Hour 48** — First place, and the first time a journalist asked to
  film it.

## Lessons learned

Scope is a weapon in a time-boxed environment. The team that won wasn't
the most ambitious one — it was the one that finished.
