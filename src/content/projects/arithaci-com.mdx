## Problem

A portfolio dies the moment it stops being updated. I wanted something
different: a home built to be added to for decades, not a project I'd
quietly abandon in eighteen months.

## Solution

An editorial, MDX-driven site with a real content system underneath it —
journal, projects, travel, photography — built to survive redesigns of
the surface without losing the archive underneath.

## Timeline

This one doesn't have an end date. Check the [changelog in the journal](/journal)
for what's new.

## Lessons learned

The hardest part of building your own website is resisting the urge to
rebuild it from scratch every time your taste changes. The content has
to outlive the design.
