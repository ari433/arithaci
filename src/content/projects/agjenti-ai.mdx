## Problem

Small businesses kept describing the same problem in different words:
too much manual, repetitive work and no engineering team to automate it
away. The tools that existed were either too generic to be useful or too
expensive to justify.

## Solution

Agjenti AI builds focused automation products — narrow enough to install
in an afternoon, capable enough to replace hours of manual work every
week. Every product starts as a conversation with one real business
before it becomes a general feature.

## Timeline

- **Month 1–2** — Interviews, throwaway prototypes, the first version
  nobody but me ever saw.
- **Month 3** — First paying customer, built around one exact problem
  they described on a call.
- **Month 6** — Rebuilt the core platform after outgrowing the first
  architecture.
- **Month 12** — A real product with a waitlist, not a favor.

## Lessons learned

The version of this product that worked was never the most technically
impressive one. It was the one that solved a problem a real person would
pay to never think about again. Read the fuller story in
