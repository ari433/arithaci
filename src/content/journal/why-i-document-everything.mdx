Most of what I've built in the last five years exists nowhere except my
own memory, which means most of it is already starting to disappear.
That's the entire reason this site exists.

## The record is the point

It's tempting to think a personal website should only show the finished,
impressive parts — the launches, the awards, the highlight reel. I've
decided to do the opposite. The unfinished projects, the failed
hackathon idea, the year I built almost nothing worth mentioning — all
of it belongs here too, because all of it is true.

## Twenty years from now

I want to open this site in twenty years and find a version of myself I
don't fully recognize anymore, saying things I've since changed my mind
about. That's not a bug. A living archive that only ever agrees with
your current opinions isn't an archive — it's a highlight reel with
extra steps.

So: everything goes here. The good ideas and the bad ones. Read the
[Now page](/now) if you want to know what that looks like today.
